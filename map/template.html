Cyprus administrative boundaries

Every district, municipality, community and parish of Cyprus, over the whole island, as drawn on the administrative map of the Department of Lands and Surveys, August 2024. Each unit is also available as its own KML file in the cyprus-admin-boundaries-kml repository.

Outlines are simplified to about 5 m for display; the KML files keep the full geometry.
